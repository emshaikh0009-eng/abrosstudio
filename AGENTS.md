# Ambros Studio Guidelines

## Communication & Team Context
- **Company & Team Identity**: Ambros Studio is a company and multidisciplinary team, not a one-person business.
- **Addressing the Team**: Treat all conversations, tasks, and requests as coming from the Ambros Studio team/company. Never address the user as "Anas" or refer to "Talk to Anas".
- **Team-First Language**: Do not assume one individual is handling every task. Naturally use "we", "our team", and "Ambros Studio" where appropriate.
